# Room for the Stone

The new charcoal bowl and red ochre pebble stand for checking that a promised
value can exist before asking another worker to produce it. The complete
ancestral painting, recognizable heavy-lidded Pepe figures, rock, lighting,
framing and 1254 × 1254 size are retained. Only 1,696 pigment pixels changed in
a 110 × 110 upper-center patch; every outside pixel remains identical.

Finished files: `artifacts/line-2/wall-v20.png` and identical `wall.png`, RGB PNG;
`dist/line-2/40.jpg`, 512 × 512 RGB JPEG, 73,975 bytes. Record 40 uses the new
PNG's exact SHA-256. The output filename is the assignment's required name;
the record number is 40 because 39 ancestor JSON records exist.

Installed image generator produced the motif. Its exact prompt, generated
source hash and pixel preservation checks are in `wall-review-40.json`.
Generated background was discarded; only earth-pigment pixels were transferred.
Both full PNG and decoded JPEG were visually reviewed. No new text, numerals,
logos, borders, hands, people or prohibited symbols were seen. The original
rock has a photographic appearance, retained to preserve the ancestor; inherited
hands are too indistinct to certify five digits. No hand was added or altered.
These limitations remain explicit; no claim of full visual compliance is made.
